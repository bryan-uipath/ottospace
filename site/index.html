AI-Native Command Center for your Agents and Apps

ottospace puts your terminals and your Mac apps in one tiled workspace. Your agents work in the same workspace and tell you when they need you.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bryan-uipath/ottospace/main/install.sh | sh

All your apps, not just the terminal

Keep a shell next to the browser it serves and the chat where the bug was reported. ottospace tiles terminals and your real Mac app windows side by side, in tabs and splits. Click an app and use it as you always do.

Stay organized and on top of your tasks

The inbox puts what needs you first, then what is running and what you saved for later. Press ⌘N and capture a task in one line, with a #project, a >repo and an @agent. Your repositories and their worktrees are one click away.

Agents are always ready to help

Your agents work in the workspace you see. An agent can open a pane, run a command and check its own result, and flag a task when it needs you. It can read what an app pane shows; it does not operate your apps.

Agents do this with the built-in ottospace ctl command. How it works is in the docs.

Install ottospace

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bryan-uipath/ottospace/main/install.sh | sh

For macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. It installs the ottospace command and osi, its installer, into ~/.local/bin, with no sudo. osi update gets the newer build.

Open source (MIT). Setup takes a few minutes: follow the quickstart.
